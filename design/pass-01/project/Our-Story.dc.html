Our Story

Since 1996, we have worked to bring you the freshest and finest Asian cuisine.

Our staff bring enthusiasm and energy to every table, and it shows in the dining experiences our guests remember.

What began as regional Shanghai and Szechuan cooking has grown into a melting pot: northern and southern Chinese dishes, Korean-style BBQ, Vietnamese and Thai influences, and Japanese sushi.

Our mission is simple: healthy choices in food and drink, a sophisticated dining atmosphere, and meals full of flavor, balance and nutrition.

The menu gathers our healthiest, tastiest and most popular dishes, from the original menu to ones inspired by customers, staff and travels.

Enjoy!

Michelle Wang Cao & Raymond Cao
